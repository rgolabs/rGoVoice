        Welcome to rGoVoice

        Create a voice, record or upload a minute of speech, train it on a free local model (XTTS-v2) or the paid ElevenLabs API, then type anything and hear it in that voice.

        1. Click + New to create a voice profile.
        2. Record with your mic or upload clean audio files.
        3. Press Train on the engine you want to use.
        4. Type text and generate. Every result is saved in the history.

        Settings

        ElevenLabs (paid engine)

        Get a key at elevenlabs.io → Profile → API keys. Instant voice cloning needs the Starter plan or higher. The key is stored locally in data/settings.json.
